# KungfuKit Público

A public collection of personas for always-on bots such as Grok Bot, Dots (ChatGPT) and Claude Duet, built only from public-domain works. Each persona is a plugin with one skill: instructions, knowledge with sources, literal evidence and limitations. The personas are generated by the KungfuKit engine, which is not part of this repository.

The repository is in English; the personas answer in the language you write in.

The structured index is in [catalog.json](catalog.json).

**Capablanca (AI)** teaches chess with the methods of José Raúl Capablanca (1888–1942), world champion from 1921 to 1927. Its sources are *Chess Fundamentals* (1921), *My Chess Career* (1920) and a 1922 article of his. It works on endings first, draws principles from simple examples, explains what each move is for and analyses games frankly, including its own earlier mistakes. It has 22 rules, each backed by literal excerpts from at least two works, and 17 knowledge notes. It has no chess engine and warns that the books' opening theory is of its time.

## Install in your bot

Each plugin has guides in `install/`. On Grok Bot, reading through GitHub and creation through the main Grok Bot were tested on real accounts with other KungfuKit personas. Dots and Duet follow the official documentation checked on 2026-10-25, untested.

- **Grok Bot**: send the prompt in `install/grok-bot.md` to the main Grok Bot (the app's default Bot, even if renamed). It fetches the files from this repository, creates a Bot just for the persona, such as "Capablanca (AI)", and hands the job over to it. Then open the new Bot and say hi. You can also create the Bot yourself and send the install message, or attach the files.
- **Claude Duet and claude.ai**: in Customize > Plugins > Add > Add marketplace, enter `novantir/kungfukit-publico` and install the plugin you want. Duet uses your account's plugins.
- **Claude Code**: `/plugin install <plugin> --marketplace novantir/kungfukit-publico`.
- **Dots (ChatGPT)**: add the repository's marketplace in the desktop app (`codex plugin marketplace add novantir/kungfukit-publico`) or use the message in `install/dots.md`.
- **Other bots with an instructions field**: paste the `SKILL.md` and attach the files in `references/`.

Install only the persona you want. Skills are called by name; do not combine personas in the same conversation by accident.

## Public domain and real people

Only works in the public domain in Brazil, the European Union, the United Kingdom and the United States are accepted. That takes two conditions:

- the author died more than 70 years ago;
- the work was first published more than 95 years ago, as US law requires. In 2026, that means works published up to 1930.

Each package records its basis in `provenance/persona.json` (`public_domain`), and the engine checks the terms. Translations and prefaces by other people carry their own rights and stay out.

The public domain frees the use of the works, but it is not an authorisation from the person or their heirs. So every persona:

- identifies itself as an AI and carries the name with "(AI)";
- never speaks as the person or invents their words or opinions;
- only puts the exact words of the works in quotation marks, with title and year.

There is no affiliation with or endorsement by heirs or organisations linked to the names.

## Layout

```text
.
├── README.md, AGENTS.md, LICENSE, catalog.json
├── .claude-plugin/marketplace.json     # Claude plugin marketplace
├── .agents/plugins/marketplace.json    # OpenAI plugin marketplace
└── plugins/<category>-<name>/
    ├── README.md, AGENTS.md, package-manifest.json, sources.json
    ├── .claude-plugin/plugin.json
    ├── .codex-plugin/plugin.json
    ├── skills/<category>-<name>/
    │   ├── SKILL.md                    # the persona
    │   ├── agents/openai.yaml
    │   └── references/                 # knowledge, evidence and profile
    ├── install/                        # grok-bot.md, dots.md, duet.md
    └── provenance/persona.json
```

Names follow `<category>-<name>`, lowercase and hyphenated, and are also the name of the skill and of the plugin.
